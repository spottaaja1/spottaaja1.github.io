Yazio Sync

Yazio Sync copies its owner's food log from Yazio into his own Google Health account.

It reads the foods in that log, together with their energy (kcal), carbohydrates, fat, and protein. It writes those nutrition entries to the owner's Google Health account through the Google Health API. It also reads the nutrition data already stored there, and uses that only to avoid adding the same entry twice.

Personal tool. Yazio Sync is a private, single-user tool. Saku runs it on his own computer. It is not offered to the public.

Why it requests Google data

The tool asks only for nutrition read and write access on the owner's Google account. Read access is used to see which nutrition entries are already in Google Health, so duplicates can be skipped. Write access is used to add the nutrition entries taken from the owner's Yazio food log. No other Google data is requested.

How that data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy. The Terms of Service explain that the tool is for personal use and is provided as is, without warranty.
